What MESK is

One exam year, five checkpoints.

MESK is the abbreviation students use for the Cambridge International Examination sat at Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools after grade 10 and grade 12. Results feed the NIS certificate, which sits across the Kazakhstani and Cambridge systems.

Preparation fails in predictable places. Talap is built around the shape of the year, not around a library of videos you will never finish.

  1. SEPDiagnostic

    A baseline in every subject you take, placed on the last published boundaries so the first number you see is honest.

  2. NOVTopic repair

    The plan narrows to the three topics losing you the most marks, with practice items pulled at your level.

  3. JANMock papers

    Full papers under exam timing. Written answers are marked against the assessment objectives, point by point.

  4. MARPast paper cycles

    Two papers a week. Every mistake goes into an error log that decides what the next cycle contains.

  5. MAYExam window

    Revision sheets built only from questions you have already got wrong, one per subject per day.

Questions

Before you sign up.

What exactly is MESK?

MESK is the abbreviation students and parents use for the Cambridge International Examination sat at Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools, after grade 10 and again after grade 12. It contributes to the NIS certificate, which combines the Kazakhstani and Cambridge systems. Talap prepares you for the papers, it does not register you for them. Your school does that.

Which subjects are covered?

Mathematics, English, First Language, Second Language and Sciences. Within Sciences you can pick Physics, Chemistry or Biology, and the practice pool changes with that choice.

Does it work in Kazakh and Russian?

Yes. The interface is available in Kazakh, Russian and English, and First Language practice is written in Kazakh or Russian rather than translated from English. The tutor answers in whichever language you ask in.

What happens to the work I submit?

Your answers are stored so the error log and the plan can work. They are not sold, and they are not used to train third party models. You can export or delete everything from your account settings. The full detail is in the privacy notice linked in the footer.

Are you affiliated with Cambridge or with NIS?

No. Talap is an independent preparation tool. It is not endorsed by, licensed by or connected to Cambridge Assessment International Education or to Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools. Past paper references point to publicly available materials.

Can a teacher run this with a whole class?

Yes, on the schools plan. Teachers get a cohort view showing where each student sits against the boundaries, which topics the class is losing marks on, and which students have stopped practising. Write to us and we will set up a pilot for one class.
